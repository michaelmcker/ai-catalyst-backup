# Coffee Chat on AI Training

Source: https://aicatalyst.biz/blog/ai-training/

## Summary

AI Catalyst’s AI training starts with one team and one real business problem. Randy Weyersberg describes mapping the workflow, deciding where AI helps and where human judgment stays in control, then building and testing an agent together on live work. The intended output is a usable set of agent instructions, supported by regular use after the session.

## FAQ

### What do participants build during AI training?

Randy describes a completed set of agent instructions for a specific piece of work, built together and tested on live work during the session.

### Which teams and delivery formats does Randy describe?

The method is adapted to marketing, sales, finance, operations and leadership groups. The Coffee Chat describes in-person sessions or live virtual delivery, customized to the team’s work.

### Where does human judgment remain in the workflow?

Before building the agent, the team maps the work step by step and identifies where AI can help and where human judgment stays in control. Randy also emphasizes using the workflow every week.
