Who is it for?

Marketing leaders and the teams responsible for briefs, creative development, analysis and commercial recommendations. We choose the exercises around their roles and current capability.

Can we use our own projects?

Yes. Agree suitable examples and information boundaries upfront, then build around the briefs, brand guidance or reports your team actually works with.

How do we know it has helped?

Agree the task your team should perform differently, the output it should produce and how that output will be reviewed. Judge the work, rather than attendance alone.
