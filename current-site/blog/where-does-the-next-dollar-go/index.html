Coffee Chat on Agentic Live Selling

Summary

AI Catalyst’s Randy Weyersberg describes two formats for agentic selling: a storefront brand agent serving an individual shopper or shopper’s agent, and a scheduled live show hosted by a brand agent for real consumers. Both formats should be built around the customer and grounded in the brand’s actual offer.

Frequently asked questions

How does one-to-one selling differ from a live show?

One-to-one selling supports an individual customer or customer’s agent. In the scheduled one-to-many format described by Randy, real consumers join a live event hosted by a brand agent.

Can an influencer’s digital twin host a selling show?

Randy describes an influencer’s twin acting as the brand agent for a scheduled launch show. The audience in that format is real people, not their shopping agents.

What business information should a storefront agent use?

The Coffee Chat names real inventory, bundles and margin. Randy’s proposed storefront agent connects the conversation to that business context rather than offering a generic search box.
