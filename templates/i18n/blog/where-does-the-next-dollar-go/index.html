Where does the next dollar go?

Randy explores brand agents, individual shopping conversations and scheduled live selling shows.

Randy Weyersberg · AI Catalyst

Watch on YouTube

Transcript

You proved it. The budget's yours. Now comes the harder question. Where does the next dollar go? Search, the front door of the internet for 20 years, is shifting. Your customer doesn't search anymore. Their agent does. 10 million people set up Meta's Muse in 6 weeks in the US and Canada alone. Amazon blocked it. Walmart and Shopify opened the door. And look at Google. Half a trillion dollars gone in a single day. Its worst day ever. Its own AI answers are eating its search ads. That's the market pricing in the end of the search box. So when your customer's agent comes knocking, who meets them? The way I look at it, the next dollar doesn't go to the search box. It goes to a brand agent on your own storefront, a digital human, your best salesperson, one-to-one around the clock, connected to your real inventory, your bundles, your margin. The experience hasn't changed in 25 years. Search box, scroll, click, the death scroll. The agent ends that. Just a conversation. And a conversation qualifies, guides, and converts. So the traffic you get is better traffic. More of what we buy happens online every year. If no one's there to meet that agent, the opportunity is gone. There's a second way in, one to many. a live show at a set time for a special launch, your brand agent hosting it, or even an influencer’s twin as your brand agent. Real people joining in. Folks love being part of an exclusive event. When the conversation happens, one-to-one or one to many, it's built around the customer. That's what drives higher consideration. The brands that build this now are the ones the agents recommend first. That's Agentic Commerce. I've spent 20 plus years as a CMO. These days, I'm focused on paying it forward, sharing what I learned with the next generation of leaders. Faster, smarter, better decisions. If that's you, let's chat or start with my AI twin on aicatalyst.biz. It knows how I think about this.
