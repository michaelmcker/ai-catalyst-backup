Prove it or lose it

Three questions to connect the whole marketing investment to the next business decision.

Randy Weyersberg · AI Catalyst

Every CMO knows this meeting. The CFO slides the budget across the table and asks one question. Prove it or lose it. My own budget was cut twice in 10 years. Once for COVID, once for a measurement battle we frankly got wrong. What's helped me since is checking whether the measurement covers the same ground as the budget. Creator spend, influencer packages, retail media, the deals that never touch a dashboard. If those are missing from the picture, it's harder to explain what the whole investment is doing. So, I come back to three questions. Can you see every spend line? Can you compare what each one contributed? And does the answer arrive in time to help with the next decision? The thing I wish I'd had then back when I had legacy MMM is what we have today. Full Commerce MMM. The real full picture of go-to-market investments. all 10 spend lines in one picture. And the third question is where most enterprises fall down. The insight arrives, but the action doesn't. Not in time to change the next decision. This is a whole new world, though. Um, an AI interface that's learned on 10 years of MMM expert context, not just Claude or ChatGPT. Ask the question in plain language, run a live simulation in the meeting, and act on it while the market is still there. That's what keeps me engaged. what's possible. Now, I've sat in that chair for 20 plus years as a CMO. These days, I'm focused on paying it forward, sharing what I learned with the next generation of leaders. The shift that helped me most is being able to sit down with finance and say, "Here's what we're seeing. Here's what we've learned, and here's where the next dollar could help us grow. Faster, smarter, better decisions." If that's you, let's chat or start with my AI twin on aicatalyst.biz. It knows how I think about this.
